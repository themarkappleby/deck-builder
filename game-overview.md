This physical card game is a co-op deck builder boss battler. What makes this game unique is the near infinite number of possible player builds. At the start of each game, each player will select their race, class and which god they will worship from a large pool of double-sided race, class and god cards. The combination of these 3 cards (and which side of each card is chosen) will define their character’s build for the session.

Components:
4 player boards
20 double-sided race cards
20 double-sided class cards
20 double-sided god cards
20 double-sided boss cards
20 🔺cards
16 🔹cards
4 🟣cards
30 other market/deck cards
30 tokens (to track various things such as remaining health and ongoing effects)

Each race, class and god card will be split in half. The top half indicates the level 1 benefit and the bottom half indicates the level 2 benefit.

Players choose a side of their race card and place it under their player board so it is partially covered and only the level 1 race portion is showing. Players also choose a side for their class and god cards but place them under their player board so they are fully covered.

Players will face 3 bosses in increasing difficulty during the course of a game. Each boss can be encountered as a level 1,  2 or 3 version, increasing the replayability. For example encountering The Witch as the 1st boss will be very different from encountering The Witch as the final boss.

The deck building cards are each made up of 1 or more symbols. The player’s player board and race, class and god cards will give these symbols meaning. For example a 🟣for one player will have a very different effect for another player.

Players’ starting decks all start with 5 🔺cards, 4 🔹cards and 1 🟣card.

Each player board indicates 🔺is 1 attack and 🔹is 1 block. This is universal and applies to all characters. The race card level 1 portion indicates 🟣X (where X is unique for each race card).

The level 2 race portion and the class and god cards may apply additional effects to 🔺🔹🟣🟩⭐️🧡symbols and/or alter the standard game rules in some other way.

On a player's turn they will draw a hand of 5 cards. They can then play, purchase or trash cards by paying their cost. The cost of a card is determined by the number of symbols on the card. For example, a card with a 🔺and 🔹symbol cost 2, and a card with 2 🔺symbols costs 2. Cards purchased go to the player’s discard pile. Cards trashed, are trashed from the players hand and removed from the game. To pay a cost, players must discard a corresponding number of cards from their hand.

There is a random market of 5 potential new cards players can purchase on their turn. When a card is purchased, immediately also discard the rightmost card, shift all the cards right and draw 2 new market cards, filling in the spaces on the left.

Each boss card has three portions. For the first boss encounter, cover the level 2 and 3 portions. For the 2nd boss encounter, cover the level 3 portion. For the 3rd boss encounter, reveal all three portions. Players win the game if they defeat the 3rd boss.

Players lose the game immediately if any player dies.

Each time players defeat a boss they can shift two of their race, class and/or god cards (or 1 card twice) down so that the next available level is visible.
